# Sharing and public pages

Most of your work stays inside your workspace. When you want someone outside to see something, you share a read-only
link or use one of Brainitive's public pages.

## Share a doc or an issue

1. Open the doc, or the issue panel, and click **Share**.
2. Choose how long the link works: **Never expires**, or **Expires in 1 day**, **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days**.
3. Click **Create link** and **Copy** it.

Anyone with the link can read that one doc or issue — nothing else: no comments, no people, no other content and no
unpublished drafts. The dialog lists every link with who created it, when, and how often it was opened. Click the
revoke button to switch a link off at any time. Once a doc or issue is shared, the button shows **Shared**.

You need edit rights on the doc or issue to share it.

## Share a plan

Open a Gantt plan and click **Share** → **Create link**. Anyone with the link can view the plan — no account needed. They
can zoom and filter, but not edit.

- **New link (old one stops working)** replaces the link.
- **Turn off link** stops sharing.

## Release notes and changelog

When you publish the release notes of a release, they get a public page, and the project gets a **Public changelog**
with all published release notes. Later edits stay private until you click **Publish update**; **Unpublish** takes the
page down. See [Boards and issues](./boards-and-issues).

## Public bookmark and snippet collections

Bookmark collections can be **Shared** (anyone with the link can view, read-only) and snippet collections or single
snippets can be **Public**. See [Bookmarks and snippets](./bookmarks-and-snippets).

## Public pages for customers

These pages are made for people outside your workspace:

| Page | Address | Who can use it |
|---|---|---|
| [Helpdesk support page](./helpdesk#support-page) | `…/support/<name>` | Anyone: submit a support request |
| [Lead form](./forms-and-leads) | `…/f/<name>` | Anyone: fill in the form |
| [Booking page](./booking-pages) | `…/book/<name>` | Anyone: book a time with you |
| [Feedback portal](./feedback-portal) | `…/feedback/<name>` | Anyone can read; sign in by email to post and vote |
| [Client portal](./client-portal) | `…/client/<name>` | Only invited clients, signed in by email |

::: tip Your legal details
Workspace admins can add the workspace's legal entity and links to its own privacy policy and imprint in the workspace
settings (**Workspace settings → Legal**). They are shown on support pages, forms and portals.
:::
