# Cloud plans and limits

Brainitive Cloud (brainitive.app) has four plans. **Every feature is in every plan.** Plans differ in how many members and projects a workspace can have, how much it can store, how long old versions are kept and which support you get.

A plan belongs to a **workspace** and is paid **per member**. Self-hosted installs have no plans and no limits; see [Licence](../self-hosting/licence).

## The plans

| | Free | Starter | Team | Business |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price per user | €0 | €3 / month or €20 / year | €5 / month or €50 / year | €15 / month or €150 / year |
| Members per workspace | 3 | 25 | unlimited | unlimited |
| Projects | 5 | 30 | unlimited | unlimited |
| Content (docs, issues, comments …) | 500 MB | 5 GB | 10 GB | unlimited |
| Files and attachments | 3 GB | 50 GB | 100 GB | unlimited |
| Version history | 7 days | last 5 forever, older ones 30 days | forever | forever |
| Support | community | community | email (2 business days) | priority (next business day), DPA, 99.9% SLA, 30-day restore |

Docs, boards, whiteboards, Gantt plans, task lists and everything else are unlimited in every plan.

## How members are counted

Everyone in the workspace counts, plus open invitations. A Free workspace has room for 3. When you invite a 4th person, Brainitive asks you to upgrade first. From then on, the workspace pays for every member. When members join or leave, the price follows automatically.

Each person can own up to 3 Free workspaces. Being a member of other people's workspaces is unlimited.

## See your plan and usage

**Workspace settings → Plan & usage** shows the plan and how much of it the workspace uses: members, projects, content and files. Storage is measured once a night. Admins see a small warning in the app when something reaches 90 %.

## At a limit

Brainitive tells you what is full and links to **Plan & billing**, where a workspace admin can upgrade. Members who aren't admins are asked to contact one. Everything already in the workspace keeps working; only adding more (members, projects, uploads, new docs or issues) waits until there is room again.

What counts and what waits at a limit:

- **Content** is everything you write: docs, issues and their comments, task lists and tasks, snippets, bookmarks, helpdesk tickets and replies, leads, Gantt plans, releases and meeting notes. When the content storage is full, nothing new of these can be added, and edits that make something longer wait too. Edits that keep the size or make it shorter, and deleting, always work, so you can clean up.
- **Files** are every upload: attachments, images in docs, bookmark files, logos and icons. A file that doesn't fit is refused before it is saved. Replacing a logo or an icon frees the old one's space; deleting an attachment frees its space at once.
- **Public forms** (the helpdesk widget, lead forms, feedback portals) show visitors "This form can't accept submissions right now." Workspace admins get one notification a day while that happens. Emails to a helpdesk are still received.
- **Very large texts** have a maximum on every plan and on self-hosted installs: 1 million characters for an issue description, snippet or task note, 10 MB for a doc, 100,000 characters for a comment.

## Downgrading or cancelling

Nothing is deleted when a workspace moves to a smaller plan, a payment fails or a subscription ends. For **90 days** the workspace keeps everything, including old versions; only adding more is paused while it is over a limit. After those 90 days, version history follows the smaller plan's rule.

## Billing

Payments are handled by **Paddle**, our reseller and merchant of record: it charges the card, adds the right VAT and sends the invoices. Workspace admins find invoices, the payment method and cancellation under **Plan & billing**.
