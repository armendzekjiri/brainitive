# Task lists

Not everything needs a board. **Lists** are simple to-do lists that work like a to-do app: type, press Enter, done. They live in a project next to boards and docs.

## Create a list

1. Click **Add content** under the project in the sidebar and choose **List**.
2. Give it a name and, optionally, a description ("What is this list for?").

## Add tasks

Type in **Task name — press Enter to add** and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The field stays open for the next task.

Date, person, repeat and reminder are one click away on each task. Drag tasks to put them in order.

- **Overdue** tasks are red, tasks due **today** are amber.
- Tick a task off to complete it. Done tasks fold away under **Completed**; click a done task's circle again to reopen it.

## Task details

Click a task to open it on the side. Everything saves as you go:

- **Notes:** add notes, links or a checklist.
- **Due date** and time, and a start date.
- **Assignee**.
- **Repeat** and **Reminder** (see below).

Use the task's menu to **Duplicate** it (the copy appears right below) or delete it. **Watch** a task to get notified about changes.

## Repeating tasks

Set **Repeat** to every day, week, month or year. When you tick off a repeating task, its due date moves to the next time instead of the task disappearing. **Doesn't repeat** turns it off.

## Reminders

Choose **At due time**, **10 minutes before**, **1 hour before** or **1 day before**. A reminder needs a due date, because it's counted back from it.

Reminders arrive in the notification bell and as a browser notification. They go to the assignee, or to the person who added the task when nobody is assigned. Times follow your own time zone.

### Morning summary

Every morning at 8:00 (your time) you get one summary of the list tasks that are due today or overdue. You can turn it off in your profile settings.

## Lists and your day

List tasks also appear in **My Work**, next to your board issues. Click the ☀ on a task to plan it into **My Day**.

## Offline

Lists keep working without internet. Your changes sync when you're back online.

## Who can do what

Project **editors** and **admins** add and edit tasks; project admins delete lists. See [Roles and permissions](./workspaces-and-projects#roles-and-permissions).
