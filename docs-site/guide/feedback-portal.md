# Feedback portal

A feedback portal is a public page where your customers share ideas, vote for the ones they want and follow what
happens to them. You decide what appears, set a status, and turn good ideas into cards on your board.

Each project can have one portal, at an address like `…/feedback/your-product`.

## Turn the portal on

You need to be a project admin.

1. Open the project and click **Feedback** in the quick links at the top.
2. Under **Settings**, tick **Portal is public**.
3. Set a **Title**, the **Public address** (the part after `/feedback/`) and an optional **Introduction**.
4. Click **Save settings**.

Use the copy button to copy the public link, or open the portal to see it as your customers do.

::: tip
Only the idea's title, description, votes and your public status note are shown publicly. Card details, comments and
assignees never appear on the portal.
:::

## What your customers do

Anyone can read the published ideas, search them, and sort by **Top** or **New**. A **Roadmap** view shows the ideas
that are **Planned**, **Building** and **Released**.

To share an idea, vote or follow, people sign in with just their email — no password and no Brainitive account:

1. They click **Sign in** (or **Sign in to share an idea**) and enter their email address.
2. Brainitive emails them a sign-in link. The link works once and for 30 minutes.
3. Back on the portal they can **Share an idea** (a title and an optional description), vote, and **Follow** an idea.

While they type a title, the portal shows **Similar ideas — vote for one instead?**, which keeps duplicates down.
New ideas are reviewed before they appear.

Followers get an email when the status of an idea changes, with a link to stop following.

## Moderate ideas

On the project's **Feedback** page, ideas are sorted into **Waiting for review**, **Published**, **Rejected** and
**Merged**. For a new idea:

- **Approve** – it appears on the portal.
- **Reject** – it stays hidden.

For published ideas you can:

- **Public status** – set Open, Planned, Building, Released or Declined, with an optional **Public note**, and click
  **Update status**. Followers get one email per status.
- **Duplicate?** – **Merge into…** another published idea. Votes and followers move to that idea.
- **Card** – **Create card** on a board, or **Link** an existing issue by its key (for example `WEB-12`), so you
  can track the work.

You get a notification when a new idea waits for review (category **Feedback portal** in your
[notification settings](./notifications)).
