# My Work, My Day and quick capture

These are your personal views: what's on your plate across every project, what you plan to do today, and a private
inbox for ideas you don't want to file yet.

## My Work

Click **My Work** in the sidebar. It lists board issues and list tasks from all your projects in one place, grouped by
project, with the board or list each one comes from, its status, priority and due date.

- **Assigned to me** – work you own.
- **Created by me** – work you created and want to keep an eye on, whoever owns it.

Use the tabs **All**, **Today**, **Overdue** and **This week** to narrow the list. Click the circle in front of a row to
mark it done (click again to reopen it).

## My Day

My Day sits at the top of My Work. It's your plan for today:

1. Click the **☀** on any issue or task (in My Work, or wherever the sun shows) to **Add to My Day**.
2. Drag items into the order you want to work on them.
3. Click the circle to mark an item done. Done items move to the bottom.

Planning never changes a task: its status, due date and assignee stay as they are. Unfinished items don't come back on
their own the next day — click **Bring over … unfinished** when you want yesterday's leftovers on today's list.

## Focus timer

Press **▶** (**Start focus timer**) on an issue in My Day. The timer shows in the top bar on every page and keeps
running across tabs and devices. Only one timer runs at a time.

When you stop it, **Log your time** opens: check the **Time worked** (change it if you took a break), the **Date**, add a
**Note** and, if your workspace uses billing activities, pick what you worked on. Click **Log time** to add it to the
issue, or **Discard** to stop without logging anything.

## Timesheet

Click **Timesheet** in the sidebar to see your week, with totals per day and for the week, and to log, edit or delete
time. See [Time tracking and billing](./time-tracking-and-billing).

## Quick capture

Got an idea in the middle of something? Press **C** anywhere in the app (not while typing), or click the capture
button in the top bar (on phones, the round button) to open **Quick capture**.

- **Idea** – the text. Markdown and code blocks are kept.
- **Link (optional)** and **Source (optional)**, e.g. "Call with Ana".
- **Organize as** – Board card, Task, Doc, Bookmark, Snippet, or **Not sure yet**.

Captures go to your private **Captures** inbox — you don't have to pick a project. Quick capture also works offline
when [offline mode](./offline) is on.

### The Captures inbox

Click **Captures** in the sidebar. Only you can see your captures. Tabs: **Inbox**, **Processed** and **Archived**, plus
a search box.

To file a capture, click **Organize**:

1. **Turn into** a board card, task, doc, bookmark or snippet.
2. Pick the **Destination** (and, for a card, the **Column**).
3. Check and edit the **Preview — edit before creating**.
4. Create it, then open the new item straight from the link.

The capture moves to **Processed**. Organizing is safe: clicking twice never creates two items, and if anything fails,
the capture stays in your inbox. You can also **Edit**, **Archive**, restore or delete captures; deleting a capture never
deletes what you already created from it.

::: tip
The Brainitive browser extension can save the page you are looking at to your captures too — see
[Apps, extension and shortcuts](./apps-and-shortcuts).
:::
