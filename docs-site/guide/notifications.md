# Notifications

Brainitive tells you when you're mentioned, assigned, when something you watch changes, and when customers write in.
You choose per topic whether a notification shows in the app, as a push notification or by email.

## The bell

The bell in the top bar shows how many unread notifications you have. It rings the moment something arrives. Click it
to open a panel with four tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Everything, grouped by Today, Yesterday, This week and Older |
| **Unread** | What you haven't read yet |
| **Mentions** | Where someone @mentioned you |
| **Assigned** | Issues, tickets and leads assigned to you |

Click a notification to open the item right where you are; it is marked as read. Hover a notification to mark it read or
unread, or archive it. **Mark all read** clears the badge. **Open inbox** opens the full page; the gear icon opens your
notification settings.

Reading a notification in one tab updates the bell in all your other tabs too.

## The inbox

Click **Inbox** in the sidebar for the full list, with the same tabs. Here you can also:

- Tick several notifications (or all loaded ones) and **Mark read**, **Mark unread** or **Archive** them at once.
- Turn on **Show archived** to see archived notifications and **Unarchive** them. Archived notifications are kept for
  90 days.

### Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `j` / `k` | Move down / up |
| `Enter` | Open |
| `e` | Archive (or unarchive) |
| `u` | Mark read / unread |
| `x` | Select |

## Choose what reaches you

Open your profile (account menu → **Profile settings**) and go to **Notifications**. The table lists every category with
three switches: **In-app**, **Push** and **Email**.

| Category | What it covers | On by default |
|---|---|---|
| **Mentions** | Someone @mentions you in an issue, comment or page | In-app, Push |
| **Assignments** | An issue, task, ticket or lead is assigned to you | In-app, Push |
| **Comments** | New comments on issues you watch | In-app |
| **Status changes** | Issues you watch move to another column or are completed | In-app |
| **Reminders & due dates** | Reminders, due soon, overdue and the daily task digest | In-app, Push |
| **Pages** | Pages you watch are published or changed | In-app |
| **Sprints** | A sprint with your issues starts or is completed | In-app |
| **Helpdesk** | New tickets, customer replies and ticket assignments | In-app, Push, Email |
| **Leads** | New leads from your forms and leads assigned to you | In-app, Push, Email |
| **Workspace** | Invitations accepted, role changes and other membership news | In-app |
| **Reviews & approvals** | Review requests, reviewer decisions, client approvals or change requests | In-app, Push, Email |
| **Feedback portal** | New ideas waiting for moderation | In-app |
| **Bookings** | New, rescheduled and cancelled bookings, and calendars that couldn't be checked | In-app, Push, Email |

Every email from Brainitive has a **Manage notifications** link at the bottom that brings you straight here.

### Morning task summary

Below the table, **Morning task summary** sends you one summary at 8:00 of your list tasks that are due today or
overdue. Click **Turn off** if you don't want it.

## Push notifications

Push notifications appear on your desktop or phone even when Brainitive isn't open.

1. In **Profile settings → Notifications**, click **Turn on** (or **Register this device**) and allow notifications
   when the browser asks.
2. Click **Send test notification** to check that it works.

Turn it on in every browser and on every device where you want notifications.

::: tip iPhone and iPad
Push notifications only work in the Home Screen app (iOS 16.4 or later): in Safari tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**,
open Brainitive from there and turn notifications on.
:::

### Your devices

**Your devices** lists every browser that receives your notifications, with when it last got one and any error.
A device that's missing here gets nothing — open Brainitive there and turn notifications on.

- **Send test** sends a test to that device. If it's accepted but nothing shows up, Brainitive lists what to check for
  Windows, macOS, iPhone and Android (for example Focus or Do not disturb).
- **Expired** means the browser dropped the subscription (after clearing site data, reinstalling or revoking
  permission). Register that browser again.
- **Remove** stops notifications to a device.

::: warning
Push notifications need a secure (HTTPS) address. On a self-hosted instance your administrator also has to set up push
keys — see [Configuration](/self-hosting/configuration).
:::

## Watching

You automatically watch issues you created or are assigned to. Use the watch option on an issue, page or task list to
follow (or stop following) other things.
