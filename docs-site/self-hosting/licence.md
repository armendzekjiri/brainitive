# Licence

Brainitive is open source: all of the code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/armendzekjiri/brainitive), and you may read it, change it and run it on your own server. It is licensed under the [Business Source License 1.1](https://github.com/armendzekjiri/brainitive/blob/main/LICENSE) (BSL). This page explains what that means in practice. The licence text itself is what counts.

## Free to self-host

You can self-host Brainitive for free, also in production and for your business, if:

- your organization has **less than €2 million annual revenue**. Group companies count together: a small subsidiary of a large group uses the group's revenue.
- or you use it **personally**, as a **registered non-profit** or as an **educational institution**. Revenue doesn't matter then.

Every feature is included. There are no user limits and no licence key.

## Agencies and IT contractors

You may install, configure and run Brainitive for a client, on the client's behalf. The free limit is checked against the **client's** revenue, not yours.

## When you need a commercial licence

- Your organization has **€2 million or more** annual revenue and uses Brainitive in production, self-hosted or not.
- You offer Brainitive, or a service based on it, to others as a **hosted, managed or subscription service**, for example a site where anyone can sign up and create their own workspaces. That competes with [Brainitive Cloud](https://www.brainitive.app/#pricing) and isn't allowed without a licence, whatever your revenue.

Testing, development and evaluation are always free. For a commercial licence, write to **hello@brainitive.app**.

## Every version becomes AGPL-3.0

Four years after a version is released, that version is also available under the **GNU AGPL-3.0**, a classic open-source licence without the conditions above.

Versions **up to and including 1.45.3** were released under the AGPL-3.0 and stay available under it. The Business Source License applies from **version 1.46.0**.
